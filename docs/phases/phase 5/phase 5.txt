Given where Entercom is today, I wouldn't jump straight to Analytics. You've reached a point where the core customer journey is solid, the recruitment module is in place, and you've already been testing with real users. The next phase should strengthen the operational side of the platform before adding new business features.

I'd recommend this order:

## Phase 1 — Complete Internal Operations (Highest Priority)

This is what your managers, staff, and technicians will use every day.

Finish:

* Technician Portal

  * Dashboard polish
  * Active jobs
  * Completed jobs
  * Earnings/history (if applicable)
  * Profile/settings
  * Mobile optimization

* Staff Portal

  * Better request management
  * Inventory management
  * Quote management
  * Better filtering
  * Notifications

* Manager Portal

  * Dashboard KPIs
  * Technician monitoring
  * Recruitment dashboard
  * Request analytics
  * Clickable cards
  * Mobile optimization

* Admin Portal

  * User management polish
  * Role management polish
  * Recruitment management
  * Inventory
  * Settings

**Goal:** Every internal role can perform its work entirely through the UI without developer intervention.

## One thing I would add before Analytics

Since you're already running **production with real users**, I would introduce a small **Production Operations** module.

This isn't a customer feature—it's for you and the client's operations team. It could include:

* Failed jobs monitor
* Email delivery monitor
* Notification monitor
* Celery queue status
* WebSocket connection status
* Recent audit events
* Background task failures
* API health indicators

This gives the team visibility into issues without needing to check Render, Railway, or logs, and it becomes increasingly valuable as usage grows.
